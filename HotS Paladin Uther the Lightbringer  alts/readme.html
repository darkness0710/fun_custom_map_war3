Heroes of the Storm Uther the Lightbringer by Blizzard Entertainment.

This pack contains:
- Uther
- Ultimate Uther
- Lumberjack Uther
- Starcraft Medic Uther
- Judgement Set Uther
- Sulfuron Uther (also wearing Judgement set; you could consider it the 'Ultimate' version of the Judgement set)

Uther's attack and spell animations are very slow so they are sped up using Retera's Model Studio.

Medic Uther has the altered Channel animation which has his Tablet facing the correct direction.

Believe it or not, regular Uther and Ultimate Uther aren't 1:1 similar enough to just use the same skin between the two. Neither are Judgement and Sulfuron Uther.

Uther was probably one of the first models made for this game and it kind of shows internally.
